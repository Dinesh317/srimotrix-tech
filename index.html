Target applications

Where it goes

High-performance applications where power density, torque density, efficiency, compactness, weight and dynamic response are critical.

  • Robotics

    Drive and actuation units for robotic joints and end effectors, where torque density and dynamic response set the design limit. TORQUE DENSITY · DYNAMIC RESPONSE

  • Automotive & EVs

    Traction and auxiliary machines developed against efficiency, weight and packaging targets rather than catalogue ratings. EFFICIENCY · WEIGHT · PACKAGING

  • Advanced Mobility

    Propulsion and lift machines for emerging platforms, where power density and weight decide the architecture before anything else does. POWER DENSITY · WEIGHT

  • Industrial Automation

    Machines for automated equipment and production systems, optimised for efficiency and sustained duty rather than peak output. EFFICIENCY · DUTY CYCLE

  • Actuation Systems

    Compact machines designed around the mechanical envelope of the mechanism they drive, not bolted on after the fact. COMPACTNESS · TORQUE DENSITY

  • Specialized Electromechanical Systems

    Low-volume and one-off machines built to requirements that no catalogue product meets. APPLICATION-SPECIFIC · LOW VOLUME

Innovation

We start with the architecture, not the catalogue.

Srimotrix Technologies Pvt. Ltd. develops cutting-edge electromagnetic systems for next-generation robotics, automotive and advanced mobility. Our core expertise is the design, analysis, prototyping and development of high-performance electric motors and generators.

Our innovation philosophy is centred on rethinking electric-machine architecture rather than working within the limits of conventional motor topologies. We investigate configurations from established radial-flux and axial-flux machines through to emerging and novel topologies, in both core and coreless forms.

The development approach integrates electromagnetic simulation, optimisation, mechanical design, thermal considerations, prototyping and experimental validation — the work of turning a novel concept into a practical engineering solution.

We aim to become a technology-driven developer of next-generation electric machines and electromagnetic systems, bridging innovative electromagnetic concepts with practical, manufacturable products for emerging industrial and mobility applications.
